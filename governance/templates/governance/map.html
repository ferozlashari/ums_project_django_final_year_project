🏛️ University Governance Map

UMS is a role-based institutional system: every office has its own people and permissions, sensitive actions need more than one person, and the system administrator runs the software without holding university authority.

1. How authority is layered

University Governance→ Role-based administration→ Faculty / Department / Office scope→ Specific permission→ Specific record (object level)→ Approval workflow→ Audit log

2. Academic lifecycle

Faculty→Department→Program→Curriculum→Course→Section→Teacher→Registration→Attendance→Assessment→Result→GPA / CGPA→Graduation

Admission lifecycle

Applicant→Program preferences (up to 5)→Eligibility→Merit→Committee selection→Offer→Admission fee→Enrolment

Finance lifecycle

Fee policy→Invoice→Challan→Payment→Receipt→Ledger→Reconciliation

Result publication (three different people)

Teacher submits marks→HOD / Dean verifies→Exam Officer approves→Controller publishes→Locked

Refund / fee adjustment

Finance Officer raises→Finance review→Finance Manager approves→VC approves if large→Cashier posts→Audit
